Update after the five-seed re-run (see also the general update)

Thank you again for the detailed code check, which shaped this re-run.

Q1 (adaptive attacks). The full adaptive suite was run on SODE-Guard with EOT-matched adversarial training (module 2). Each attack is run once against fixed Brownian paths (seeds 0-7, a deterministic target) and once against fresh paths on every call:
- PGD-40 and PGD-100 with restarts
- APGD-CE and APGD-DLR with EOT
- EOT-PGD (EOT 8)
- a 64-path expected-margin attack
- Square (black box)
- transfer from two surrogates

Worst-case macro-F1 over all attacks (5 seeds):
- fixed paths: 0.420 ± 0.010
- fresh paths: 0.412 ± 0.012
- without module 2: 0.245 (+0.174 with module 2, Holm-adjusted p = 0.016)

Individual attacks land between 0.42 and 0.45, so no single attack dominates.

The masking checks pass:
- Varying eps: 0.462 at 0.01, 0.420 at 0.03, 0.290 at 0.1, 0.058 at 0.3, 0.004 at 1.0.
- Varying steps: 0.425 at 10, 0.423 at 40, 0.427 at 100.
- The black-box and transfer attacks are weaker than the white-box ones.

A full-strength re-check of seed 0 (PGD-200 with 5 restarts, EOT-32, a 256-path expected-margin attack, Square with 5,000 queries) is running now and will be reported in the revision.

Q2 (code version). There is now a single released pipeline covering data preparation, training, attacks, certification and aggregation:
- What attacks differentiate through: the deployed predictor, i.e. the 8-path mean logits with the post-hoc logit adjustment. Every attack differentiates through exactly that predictor, by ordinary backpropagation through the Euler-Maruyama scheme.
- Training randomness: Brownian seeds are fresh in every training step.
- Data hygiene: duplicates and conflicting-label flows are removed before splitting.
- Statistics: every number in the update is a mean over five seeds with a 95% CI. Comparisons use a paired bootstrap with Holm correction.

Q3 (regulariser and Proposition 2). As we reported, the regulariser had no measurable effect, and it is not part of the revised method. It is replaced by three modules, each with a significant measured effect:
- imbalance-aware training: clean macro-F1 0.438 to 0.495;
- EOT-matched adversarial training: worst case 0.245 to 0.420;
- a smoothing certificate over the SDE's own noise: certified accuracy at l2 radius 0.273 from 0.096 (Theorem A) to 0.556.

Theorem A stays in the paper as a deterministic finite-step certificate. The headline certificate becomes the smoothing certificate, which samples input noise and Brownian paths jointly and is on par with a smoothed MLP (0.556 vs 0.555).
