Update: the protocol now covers three corpora, and the recipe's gains carry over to all of them.

We repeated the full five-seed protocol on UNSW-NB15 and CSE-CIC-IDS2018. Both come from networks and feature extractors different from the ICS3D container flows. On every corpus, duplicates, conflicting-label flows and integration metadata are removed before splitting.

ICS3D containers / UNSW-NB15 / CSE-CIC-IDS2018:
- Imbalance-aware training, clean macro-F1: +0.057 / +0.144 / +0.016 (all significant).
- EOT-matched adversarial training, worst-case macro-F1: +0.174 / +0.149 / +0.166 (all significant).
- SODE-Guard vs adversarially trained MLP, worst case: -0.001 / +0.011 (significant) / +0.009. It is level or ahead on every corpus.
- SODE-Guard vs smoothed MLP, certified accuracy at l2 radius 0.273: +0.006 / +0.020 (significant) / -0.017 (significant). It stays within 0.02 of the best smoothed baseline.
- Gradient-boosted trees, the most accurate clean models, collapse under attack on every corpus: worst case 0.02-0.14, and 0.05-0.33 under feature-constrained attacks.
- Adversarially trained SODE-Guard keeps 0.446 / 0.571 / 0.893 under constrained attacks.
- Conformal alarm thresholds stay close to the chosen budget: 0.5-0.9% at a 1% budget and 4.9-5.5% at a 5% budget.

We also document a leakage case study. An integration column (file size per daily capture file) had reversed the certification comparison on CSE-CIC-IDS2018; removing it turned a spurious significant lead into the deficit reported above.

The results, code and per-seed files are in the anonymised repository.
